About BILLPATRON

BILLPATRON is a platform concept founded to bridge the gap between people who need help paying legitimate essential bills and people who are willing and able to assist.

🧾

Essential bills

People can submit outstanding electricity, water, rent, school, hospital, internet and other legitimate bills for review.

🛡️

Verification first

Submissions must be checked before publication. Sensitive personal details and account identifiers should never be exposed in the public gallery.

🤝

Direct sponsorship

Donors choose a verified bill and pay the relevant service provider directly—not the recipient and not BILLPATRON.

Founded & owned by Joseph Maina Ndiritu

For donors and sponsors

Browse verified needs, choose a bill you wish to support, and settle it directly with the relevant provider using that provider’s official payment channel.

🔎

Review verified bills

See the bill category, amount requested and limited non-sensitive details approved for public display.

💳

Pay the provider directly

Do not send sponsorship money to a recipient or into BILLPATRON’s account. Confirm payment details with the provider.

📋

Confirm sponsorship

Where available, provide a payment confirmation reference so the platform can update the sponsorship record.

Important: BILLPATRON does not hold donor funds or guarantee that a selected bill has been paid until settlement is confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Will every submitted bill be sponsored?

No. Verification allows a bill to be considered; it does not guarantee that a donor will choose it.

Do donors pay BILLPATRON?

No. Donors pay the service provider directly. BILLPATRON’s proposed subscription is for recipients who choose a paid membership.

Can I submit any amount?

There is no fixed amount limit in the proposed model, but every submission must be legitimate and verifiable.

Which bills are eligible?

Electricity, water, rent, school fees, hospital bills, internet and other legitimate essential bills may be considered.

How are bills verified?

The platform team must review supporting evidence privately and publish only the minimum necessary redacted information.

Is BILLPATRON live?

This page is a design/embed starter. Accounts, secure uploads, verification, notifications and payment processing require a connected production backend.
